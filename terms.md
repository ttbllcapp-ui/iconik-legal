Iconik is provided **as is**. We work to keep it reliable, but we can't
guarantee it will be free of errors or available at all times.

Several things are controlled by iOS, not by us, and are outside our control:

- **How often widgets refresh.** iOS decides. A clock may lag behind by a
  minute, and battery, weather, calendar or step data may be a few minutes old.
- **Custom app icons.** iOS provides no way for one app to replace another
  app's icon. Icons you make here are used through Apple's Shortcuts app, which
  creates a shortcut rather than replacing the real icon.
- **Wallpapers.** iOS does not allow an app to set your wallpaper directly, so
  Iconik saves the image and you apply it from Photos.

We describe these limits inside the app under **Settings → How it works**.
